03 / METHODHOW WE WORK

Measured
then driven.

The method keeps the map faithful to Hong Kong while making every change testable in Assetto Corsa.

01

Reference

Collect satellite, road, elevation and street-level references for the area.

02

Build

Translate the references into terrain, roads, junctions, buildings and materials.

03

Test

Load the area in-game, drive it, check scale and record anything that breaks.

04

Improve

Move the work to the right status, fix the rough edges and repeat the loop.
